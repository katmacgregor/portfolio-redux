Creative Technology

03 / Senior Software Engineer · 2016

I helped establish a newly formed innovation group named Creative Technology, Disney Interactive's incubator for interactive web experiences.

Our team of 3 built responsive, mobile-first experiences across Disney's portfolio of sites while expanding our development toolkit beyond the existing Ruby-based stack, working with SVG, Phaser, Nunjucks, and the broader Bower, Gulp, and npm ecosystem to build flexible interactive systems and game engines that could be reused, configured, and scaled across properties.

CT Branding

I created the Creative Technology team's branding and portfolio site, extending the logic of Disney Interactive's rainbow geometric Mickey logo. I translated those principles into a system that felt part of the same family while giving Creative Technology its own playful character, reflecting the flexibility and adaptability of the work we created.

Game Engines

Prior to this team, my work captured a single moment of delight, like Star Wars' lightsaber toggle. That evolved into building reusable mechanics that could scale across Disney properties. We built engines for Disney LOL, including Finding Dory path tracing, Hyperspace Dash, Tsum Tsum match-3, and memory-flip card games, designing each mechanic and adapting its visual treatment to the individual property.

Activity Engines

I developed a digital coloring book tool to transform Disney Publishing's existing print artwork into responsive SVGs that could be painted with familiar tools like paint buckets and airbrushes. Images, colors, tools, and translations could all be configured through the CMS, allowing the same engine to automatically process different coloring book pages without rebuilding the experience.

Takeaways

Every experience shared the same foundation: CMS configuration, theming, and analytics built in, allowing producers to quickly launch and customize new experiences. We built the underlying mechanics once, while keeping each experience unique to its property. Instead of measuring what I could build myself, I began looking for ways to give others the tools and systems to apply and enhance the work themselves. This methodology has carried forward into my current design approach, where templated UX specs and system patterns help teams create at scale.
